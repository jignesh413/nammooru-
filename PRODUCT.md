# Product Context: Nammooru (Cinematic Editorial V2)

## 1. Product Thesis
**Nammooru** (ನಮ್ಮೂರು — *"Our City / Our Town"*) is a mobile-first digital companion designed specifically for newcomers living in Bengaluru who want to bridge the linguistic divide and connect with residents in everyday Kannada.

Rather than teaching Kannada as an abstract academic syllabus, Nammooru grounds language learning in immediate physical reality:
1. **Camera Discovery (Photo)**: Point your camera at real-world objects around Bengaluru to immediately discover their authentic Kannada name, roman transliteration, and pronunciation.
2. **Spacious Editorial Learning (Learn)**: Understand vocabulary and spoken phrases one at a time on warm, readable pearl surfaces, backed by authentic audio.
3. **Encouraging Practice (My Tries & Achievements)**: Log real conversation attempts without fear—celebrating every effort, whether it went smoothly, needed help, or froze.
4. **Supportive Community (Transform)**: A profile-first community space to share triumphs, seek advice, and learn from fellow newcomers.

---

## 2. Global Navigation Architecture (3 Primary Destinations)
1. **Photo** — Primary focal camera discovering Bengaluru objects.
2. **Learn** — Spacious learning hub organizing 6 dedicated destinations:
   - **Discover Words**: Kannada script (vowels & consonants) and 51 categorized vocabulary words.
   - **Useful Phrases**: 22 conversational real-world Bengaluru phrases with audio playback.
   - **Word Garden**: Personal collection of saved words and recognized items.
   - **My Real-Life Tries**: Record conversation attempts across 3 non-judgmental outcomes (*Went well*, *Needed help*, *Froze in the moment*).
   - **My Achievements**: 9 transparent badges evaluated against real-world attempts with verifiable supporting records.
   - **My Progress**: Personal milestones, tries count, and outcome breakdown.
3. **Transform** — Profile-first community space centered around the member's journey, words learned, tries logged, and shared discussions.

---

## 3. Brand & Content Integrity Commitments
- **Exact Brand Name**: "Nammooru" (pure typography; no logo mark, monogram, emblem, or symbol).
- **Interface Language**: All system UI chrome, navigation labels, and instructions are 100% English.
- **Authentic Kannada**: Kannada script is strictly reserved for genuine learning content (letters, vocabulary words, transliterations, and spoken phrases).
- **Zero Gamification Slop**: No competitive streaks, points, leaderboards, or vanity rankings.
- **Privacy & Local Persistence**: Stores user data securely in `localStorage` (`nammooru_garden_v1`, `nammooru_attempts_v1`, `nammooru_community_v1`, `nammooru_profile_v1`).
