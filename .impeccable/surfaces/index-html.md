# Surface Brief: Nammooru Cinematic Editorial Web Redesign V2

## Scope
Main application shell covering 3 primary navigation destinations: Photo, Learn (with 6 dedicated subpages migrating Innovate), and Transform (profile-first community), plus modal sheets.

## Visitor Mode
operate

## Direction Contract

### THESIS
A cinematic, warm-pearl editorial companion that turns everyday Bengaluru Kannada communication into confident action. Replaces dark unreadable gloom with warm ivory and pearl surfaces for content, near-black ink typography for effortless legibility, selective Playfair Display italic accents, deep burgundy and teal atmospheric illumination, a hero optical camera aperture, and a 3-destination floating dock.

### OWN-WORLD
Warm ivory (#F7F3EE) and soft pearl (#EEE8E3) readable content cards; deep atmospheric canvas (#0D1115) with soft burgundy (#642E39) and deep teal (#174F4D) ambient illumination; near-black primary ink (#202124) for reading comfort; Helvetica-based primary type with Playfair Display Italic expressive accents and Noto Sans Kannada; floating 3-destination navigation dock.

### STORY
Newcomers open Nammooru to discover everyday Kannada objects through a heroic full-screen viewfinder, explore authentic vocabulary and spoken phrases at their own pace in dedicated spacious subpages, courageously record real conversations without fear of judgment, and connect with a supportive community through their personal profile.

### FIRST VIEWPORT
Clean editorial header with "Nammooru" wordmark and discrete Bengaluru status badge; heroic tall optical camera viewfinder aperture with corner reticles, rotating ring, and high-contrast tactile white pill Shutter button; floating bottom dock with focal Photo anchor, vertical divider, and grouped Learn and Transform tabs.

### FORM
Cinematic editorial mobile web interface, warm pearl readability, restrained red-and-teal illumination, and disciplined spacing.

### FINISH
0 anti-patterns in Impeccable detect, zero broken images, zero cramped padding, full test suite pass, verified on mobile (390x844) and desktop viewports with updated DESIGN.md.
